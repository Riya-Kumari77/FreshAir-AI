# FreshAir-AI

### Your AI Companion for Cleaner Air and Healthier Living

FreshAir-AI is a Generative AI-powered Urban Air Quality Assistant developed for **PromptWars Virtual – Challenge 6**.

It aims to help families and communities navigate severe winter smog and urban air pollution through personalized recommendations, real-time air-quality information, indoor air improvement guidance, and sustainable daily routines.

## Problem Statement

Urban air pollution and winter smog create challenges for families and communities. People often struggle to understand air-quality conditions, identify practical ways to protect themselves, and make environmentally responsible daily decisions.

FreshAir-AI aims to address this problem through an intelligent, location-aware assistant.

## Our Solution

FreshAir-AI combines live environmental information with Generative AI to provide practical, personalized and understandable recommendations.

The application is designed to help users:
- Understand local air-quality conditions.
- Follow safer daily routines during periods of high pollution.
- Improve indoor air quality.
- Explore low-emission transportation alternatives.
- Make sustainable lifestyle choices.
- Understand the estimated environmental impact of their decisions.

## Planned Features

1. **Live AQI Dashboard** – Air Quality Index, available pollutant readings and pollution trends.
2. **AI Air Quality Assistant** – Conversational assistance powered by Gemini.
3. **Smart Daily Routine Planner** – Personalized morning, afternoon and evening recommendations.
4. **Indoor Air Quality Guide** – Practical indoor pollution reduction strategies.
5. **Sustainable Alternatives** – Low-emission commuting and lifestyle recommendations.
6. **Environmental Impact Tracker** – Estimated emissions savings from sustainable choices.
7. **User Personalization** – Location, lifestyle and family preferences.
8. **Multilingual Support** – Accessible recommendations in English and Hindi.

## Technology Stack (Proposed)

- Frontend: React.js, Vite, Tailwind CSS
- Backend: Node.js, Express.js
- Generative AI: Google Gemini API
- Database: MongoDB Atlas
- Authentication: JWT and bcrypt
- Data Visualization: Recharts
- Environmental Data: External Air Quality and Weather APIs
- UI/UX Design: Google Stitch
- AI Development: Google Antigravity and Google AI Studio
- Version Control: Git and GitHub

## How It Will Work

1. Users select their location and provide relevant lifestyle preferences.
2. The application retrieves available environmental information from external data providers.
3. The backend processes the air-quality information.
4. Gemini receives verified environmental context and user preferences.
5. The AI generates personalized recommendations.
6. Users can explore routines, indoor air-quality guidance and sustainable alternatives.

**Important:** Environmental measurements will come from external data providers. Generative AI will interpret the information rather than inventing air-quality readings.

## Approach and Logic

- Location-aware recommendations.
- Context-based AI prompting.
- Data-driven pollution awareness.
- Personalized lifestyle suggestions.
- Transparent environmental impact estimates.
- Secure handling of API keys and user information.
- Responsive and accessible user interface.

## Assumptions

- Users have access to an internet connection for live environmental information.
- Air-quality data availability depends on the selected location and external providers.
- AI recommendations are general informational guidance, not medical diagnoses.
- Environmental impact calculations will use documented assumptions and estimates.
